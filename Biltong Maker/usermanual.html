Biltong Maker - User Manual

This manual explains how to use the Biltong Maker dashboard to monitor temperature and humidity and control the automatic settings.

Back to Dashboard

1. Monitoring Temperature and Humidity

The dashboard displays the current temperature and humidity readings from the sensors inside the biltong maker.

  • Temperature: Displays the current temperature in degrees Celsius (°C).
  • Humidity: Displays the current humidity as a percentage (%).
  • Progress circles: Visually represent the current sensor readings.
  • Safe range: The indicator shows the configured minimum and maximum thresholds.

2. Adjusting the Temperature and Humidity Ranges

Each reading has controls that allow you to adjust its minimum and maximum thresholds.

  1. Locate the controls beside the temperature or humidity display.
  2. Use the buttons to increase or decrease the minimum and maximum values.
  3. Check the displayed range to confirm your selected thresholds.
  4. Your changes are sent to the ESP32 automatically.

Temperature thresholds are measured in °C, while humidity thresholds are measured as percentages.

3. Automatic Control

The automatic control toggle enables or disables the system's automatic management of the heat lamp and fan.

  • Enabled: The ESP32 automatically controls the heat lamp and fan using the configured temperature and humidity thresholds.
  • Disabled: Automatic control is switched off, and the system turns off the heat lamp and fan.

When automatic control is enabled, the heat lamp responds to temperature and the fan responds to humidity.
